# Evidence and appendix source audit

Verified on 2026-11-10. Entries prefixed ev_ belong to Chapters 12-13 and Appendices A-D. Source-derived factual summaries are intentionally brief; the extended inference audits, counterexamples, derivations, and proposed validations are original pedagogical analysis rather than claims that the papers performed those tests.

## Primary neuroscience and methodological papers

- **ev_chaudhuri2019** — Chaudhuri, Gercek, Pandey, Peyrache, Fiete. Nature Neuroscience 22, 1512-1520 (2019). DOI 10.1038/s41593-019-0460-x. Publisher metadata and author-hosted full PDF verified: https://mcgovern.mit.edu/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/s41593-019-0460-x.pdf . Read Fig. 3 and surrounding text, particularly inward off-manifold flux. **Important:** it would be inaccurate to say this study inferred attraction from only a ring image. It also evaluates dynamics. The paper's representational premise about matching variable/manifold topology requires encoding assumptions; chapter explains this as an inference bridge rather than alleging an empirical error.
- **ev_gardner2022** — Full author list, title, venue, year, pages and DOI verified against publisher/PubMed and full PMC article: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8810387/ . DOI 10.1038/s41586-021-04268-7, Nature 602, 123-128 (2022). Module-level toroidal organization and cross-state/environment evidence checked. Chapter does not claim all feedforward alternatives were ruled out.
- **ev_sadtler2014** — Publisher and PubMed metadata verified: https://www.nature.com/articles/nature13665 ; https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25164754/ . Nature 512, 423-426 (2014). Full authors verified. BCI mapping and operational intrinsic-manifold interpretation; do not generalize a tested timescale to permanent inability.
- **ev_oby2019** — Primary publisher/author PDF and PubMed: https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1820296116 ; https://users.ece.cmu.edu/~byronyu/papers/ObyPNAS2019.pdf ; https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31182595/ . PNAS 116(30), 15210-15215 (2019). Full eight-author list verified. Used as a later extension of short-timescale repertoire constraints.
- **ev_churchland2012** — Publisher metadata and author-hosted primary PDF: https://www.nature.com/articles/nature11129 ; https://stat.columbia.edu/~cunningham/pdf/ChurchlandNature2012.pdf . Nature 487, 51-56 (2012). Full seven-author list verified. Do not attribute a stable-limit-cycle assertion to the original study.
- **ev_elsayed2017** — Publisher page and PubMed verified: https://www.nature.com/articles/nn.4617 ; https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28783140/ . Nature Neuroscience 20, 1310-1318 (2017), DOI 10.1038/nn.4617. Publisher figure list explicitly identifies Fig. 6 as motor population dynamics not being an expected byproduct. Thus this chapter does not miscite the paper as refuting jPCA rotations. Structured null-model principle is the relevant contribution.
- **ev_kalidindi2021** — Full primary eLife article read: https://elifesciences.org/articles/67256 . Full seven-author list and citation block verified: eLife 10:e67256 (2021), DOI 10.7554/eLife.67256. Rotations can arise through feedback in controllers lacking intrinsic recurrence; this is a constructive alternative, not proof that cortex lacks recurrence.
- **ev_sabatini2024** — Publisher and PubMed metadata/abstract/introduction verified: https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-024-51308-7 ; https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39143078/ . David A. Sabatini and Matthew T. Kaufman, Nature Communications 15, 7007 (2024), DOI 10.1038/s41467-024-51308-7. Different reaches have different rotational planes; used as a refinement of a shared-plane approximation, not evidence that rotations disappear.
- **ev_gallego2020** — Publisher/PubMed/PMC indexed primary text verified: https://www.nature.com/articles/s41593-019-0555-4 ; https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7007364/ . - **ev_sussillo2013** — PubMed metadata and author-hosted full paper read: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23272922/ ; https://barak.net.technion.ac.il/files/2012/11/sussillo_barak-neco.pdf . - **ev_efron1979** — Metadata verified through primary DOI/publisher record: https://doi.org/10.1214/aos/1176344552 . The Annals of Statistics 7(1), 1-26 (1979). Generic bootstrap explanation uses standard probability and original examples.

## Local mathematical checks

- Finite-sample manifold interpolation proof is constructive and applies to any finite set of distinct points in R^p, p >= 2.
- The F_2 Betti-number counterexample comparing torus and Klein bottle is intentional; over other coefficients their homology differs.
- The constant-hidden-state apparent-return derivation conditions on noisy observations and does not describe a latent restoring mechanism.
- All matrix-gradient shapes, rank-nullity formulas, Gaussian posterior precisions, Euler stability bound, and Ornstein-Uhlenbeck variance have been checked algebraically.
- No source figures or long passages were reproduced. No invented experimental sample counts are used.
