## Mathematical checks made during authoring

- PCA reconstruction proof handles arbitrary rank-k matrices via their row spaces; covariance requires centering and (n-1) normalization.
- Parabolic example covariance is diag(1/3,4/45), first-PC fraction 15/19.
- Continuous stability uses negative real parts; discrete stability uses unit-modulus bounds. Lyapunov proof supplies a quadratic integral construction.
- Hopf paragraph separates stability on the center manifold from full-space orbital attraction, requiring remaining eigenvalues to be strictly negative for the latter.
- Attractor convention does not silently classify an unbounded line of equilibria as a compact attractor.
- Rips filtration is edge distance <= epsilon; Cech uses ball radius. Four unit-circle cardinal points have H1 interval [sqrt(2),2).
- Boundary matrices are computed over F2; filled triangles kill cycles; H_k computation needs simplices through dimension k+1.
- Circle-coordinate construction explicitly requires an integral cocycle; finite-field persistence alone is insufficient.
- Curved stable manifold y=x^2/3, center expansion y=x^2+2x^4+O(x^6), and slow graph y=x/(1-epsilon) were algebraically checked.
- Observation-space closure condition distinguishes static low-dimensional geometry from a sufficient dynamical state.
